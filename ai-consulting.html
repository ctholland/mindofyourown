The Opportunity

More capability.
More authorship.

AI changes what work looks like. It also changes what it means to be human at work. The aim is not merely to use the tools. It is to build practical fluency while keeping judgment, context, and authorship human.

A Human-Centered Working Method

AIM. ASSIGN. ASSESS. OWN.

A practical discipline for using AI without outsourcing the thinking that matters.

01

AIM

Start with the human objective. Decide what you are actually trying to understand, make, change, or solve before the tool enters the process.

Purpose · Context · Direction
02

ASSIGN

Give the system a defined job. Set the role, relevant context, constraints, source material, and what a useful result needs to do.

Role · Inputs · Constraints
03

ASSESS

Interrogate the output. Check evidence, assumptions, omissions, fit, consequences, and whether the result actually serves the original aim.

Evidence · Judgment · Fit
04

OWN

Keep the final judgment human. You decide what gets accepted, changed, rejected, published, implemented, or acted on.

Authorship · Accountability · Agency
“AI systems were built on humanity, so it is imperative we keep the human at the center.”
