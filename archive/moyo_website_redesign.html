About

C.T. Holland is an artist, writer, and long-time student of consciousness, language, persuasion, identity, and human behavior.

With nearly three decades of experience in Neuro-Linguistic Programming, Ericksonian Hypnotherapy, and advanced NLP techniques, he brings deep technical knowledge with genuine care for each person's unique journey toward positive change.

This lifelong work gave rise to a parallel project developed over 25+ years: Mind of Your Own (MOYO) — a system for rethinking identity, perception, and the unconscious patterns that shape our lives.

MOYO draws from the legacy of thinkers like G.I. Gurdjieff, P.D. Ouspensky, Julian Jaynes, and Ernest Becker, integrating their wake-up calls with the practical rewiring tools of NLP. It's not a belief system — it's a framework for building a self you can actually live with.

— C.T. Holland
