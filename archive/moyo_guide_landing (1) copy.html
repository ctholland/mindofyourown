ABOUT THE GUIDE

A documented field guide to the structures shaping American democracy, public knowledge, political power, and civic life.

Mind Of Your Own® was never meant to be a book about one politician, one party, or one controversy — it's about how people come to believe what they believe. The full Mind Of Your Own® book is currently in development. This Guide is an early Special Publication that applies those ideas to the political moment we are living through now, where abstraction is no longer enough. It documents representative case studies in civic manipulation — across health, education, environment, religion, and immigration — not to catalog every event, but to make the underlying structures visible.
