WHY THIS GUIDE EXISTS

In an ideal world, this Guide would not exist.

Mind Of Your Own® was never meant to be a book about one politician, one party, or one controversy — it's about how people come to believe what they believe, how institutions shape perception, and how we become authors, or surrender authorship, of our own minds. The full Mind Of Your Own® book is currently in development. This Guide is an early Special Publication that applies those ideas to the political moment we are living through now, where abstraction is no longer enough.
