Coaching

There is somewhere you want to go.

We work together to move you toward it.

Coaching is goal-directed. You bring a decision, a transition, a pattern you want to change, a challenge you want to move through. We work with direction and intention.

  • Transitions & decisions
  • Communication & recurring patterns
  • Creative blocks or direction
  • Identity and role shifts
  • Turning insight into actual movement
Conversation

There doesn’t have to be somewhere to go.

Sometimes you simply want an intelligent, engaged human being to talk with.

A real person. Present. Listening. No feed. No algorithm. No agenda required. The product is human attention — which is becoming considerably harder to find.

  • Something is on your mind
  • You want to think aloud with someone paying attention
  • You want perspective without making it a formal goal
  • You want a conversation that goes where it needs to go

Who you’re talking to

Three decades of listening at a different level.

C.T. Holland has spent roughly thirty years studying and working with communication, NLP, language, behavior, coaching, creativity, persuasion, and the structures people use to make meaning. That background informs the quality of the conversation — without turning every conversation into a coaching session.

The difference is not that every session becomes clinical. It is that the person on the other end is capable of listening closely and engaging with depth when the conversation calls for it.

How sessions work

Booked access. Not open-ended availability.

Sessions are scheduled and prepaid. You choose the kind of session, select an available time, pay, and we talk. Audio-first — no need to perform for a camera. Conversation is more focused that way.

This is not a hotline. It is not an always-on service. The structure keeps the sessions what they are meant to be.
