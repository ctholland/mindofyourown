Who you’re talking to

Thirty years spent studying how people communicate, think, change, and make meaning.

C.T. Holland has spent roughly thirty years studying and working with communication, NLP, language, behavior, coaching, creativity, persuasion, and the structures people use to make meaning. That background informs the quality of the conversation — without turning every conversation into a coaching session.

The difference is not that every session becomes clinical. It is that the person on the other end is capable of listening closely and engaging with depth when the conversation calls for it.

How sessions work

Scheduled sessions. Clear boundaries.

Sessions are booked in advance and prepaid. Choose your format, select an available time, and we’ll connect audio-first, with video available if preferred. The structure protects the quality of the conversation and keeps the time focused.

Why audio-first?

Video can add a second layer of self-consciousness. Audio can make a conversation feel less performative and make it easier to focus on the conversation itself. Video is always available if you prefer it.
