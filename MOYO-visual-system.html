Rule of one: Use one primary emphasis treatment per asset. For MOYO Core and Questions / Signal, that may be the teal/cyan highlight bar. For Systems / Democracy, use one restrained cobalt emphasis treatment — blue type, italic emphasis, a rule, or other Guide-compatible device. Not every word. Not alternating accents. One. Do not scatter multiple competing treatments through one composition — the restraint is what gives it power.

Portrait / identity rule — approved assets only: C.T. Holland's approved supplied photographs and cutouts are fixed source assets. Do not regenerate his face, reconstruct his head, alter skull shape, reinterpret facial geometry, or create a substitute AI portrait when an approved portrait already exists. For production: select from the approved portrait library, then crop, scale, position, color-grade, mask/cut out, or add background lighting or glow around the portrait if needed — but preserve the actual supplied face/head geometry. "Using an approved portrait asset" and "generating a new portrait" are different things. Only the former is part of the standard workflow.

02 — Content Lanes

Three lanes. One family.

01
MOYO Core
Identity · Authorship · Human Potential
The deepest layer. Who you are, how you think, what you can become. NLP, consciousness, language, the self as construction. Intimate. Warm. Portrait-forward. This is the personal transmission.
Visual signature Dark navy / near-black foundation. Bold white sans headline — same family as Questions / Signal. Teal/cyan accent, used sparingly as a highlight bar. Warmth comes from environmental lighting, wood, lamps, skin tone, books, interiors — not from a formal amber graphic accent. Human, editorial, direct, authored.
02
Systems / Democracy
Institutions · Power · Civic Literacy
The structural layer. Understand the systems, see the patterns, participate more effectively. Civic literacy, media, money, institutions, power structures. More architectural than personal.
Visual signature Cobalt blue accent. Geometric, monumental imagery — architecture, newspapers, infrastructure. Feels like looking at a structure from the outside. Cooler. More analytical.
03
Questions / Signal
Provocation · Awareness · Shareable Ideas
The signal layer. Questions that interrupt, open minds, and start better conversations. Most minimal. Most immediate. Designed to stop a scroll. The question IS the product.
Visual signature Teal accent (primary). Darkest background. Boldest type. Most space around the question. Portrait appears less frequently — sometimes the question alone. Highest contrast of the three.

How they differ while staying in family: MOYO Core and Questions / Signal share the dark foundation, grid texture, bold sans headline treatment, and teal accent family — Core reads warmer and more intimate through its photography and environmental lighting, Questions reads starker and more minimal through contrast and restraint, not through a different accent color. Systems / Democracy is allowed to break the dark field — it runs in the existing light, civic/editorial world, with cobalt as the connective thread back to the rest of the system.

Podcast is a delivery layer, not a fourth lane. Every episode still belongs to one of the three lanes above — Core, Systems, or Questions — and inherits that lane's accent and image treatment. The podcast frame (series label, episode number, brand mark, bottom bar) stays constant across all three; what changes episode to episode is which lane it's speaking from.

04 — Template Recipe

How to build one episode's assets.

Same inputs every time. Swap the question and the highlight phrase, select an approved portrait from the library. Everything else stays fixed. That's how a recognizable series works.

1

The Question

The episode's central question or provocation, in bold lowercase. 3–8 words. The thing that makes someone pause.

2

The Highlight

One phrase gets the emphasis treatment. Core and Questions / Signal: teal bar — Questions gets the strongest use of it. Systems / Democracy: cobalt emphasis instead (blue type, italic, or a rule), no requirement for a teal rectangle. Usually the most unexpected 2–3 words. Never the whole question. It tells the eye where to look.

3

The Portrait

Right-side anchor. SELECT an approved portrait from the C.T. Holland portrait library — do not generate a new one. Clean background. Facing slightly toward the type. Crop, scale, position, and color-grade as needed, but preserve the actual supplied face/head geometry. Not every episode needs the portrait — Question-only works for the strongest lines.

4

Series Label

The label reflects which lane the episode belongs to — "MOYO Core," "Systems / Democracy," or "Questions / Signal" — or simply "Podcast" as a neutral default. Not a fixed show title. Oswald at 10–11px, top-left, cobalt or white at 40% opacity. Present but not competing.

5

Field Texture

Subtle grid lines. 1–3 crosshair markers. Optional glow/orb behind portrait or top-right. All at low opacity. Atmospheric, not decorative.

6

Bottom Bar + Brand Mark

Accent bar, full-width, 3–4px — teal/cyan for Core and Questions / Signal, cobalt/blue for Systems / Democracy. The actual Mind Of Your Own® logo bottom-left — never redrawn or recolored. Episode number bottom-right if applicable. Never prominent.
