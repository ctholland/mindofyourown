In an ideal world, this Guide would not exist.

Mind Of Your Own® was never meant to be about one politician, one party, or one controversy. It's about how people come to believe what they believe — how institutions shape perception, language frames reality, and we become authors, or surrender authorship, of our own minds. A book about structure, not content.

Look beyond the content of a story and examine its structure. The names change. The patterns don't.

This Guide exists because the moment demands examples, documented in real time. It isn't an encyclopedia — it doesn't catalog every event or policy. These pages present representative examples: evidence that makes invisible systems visible.

The point isn't to memorize hundreds of stories. The point is to recognize the handful of structures that keep producing them.
