What this is

Thinking aloud.
Working in public.

The podcast turns work already being done into public thought. One mechanism at a time. One story. One framework. One question that doesn't resolve the way you expect.

Not doctrine. Not a five-step program. Something closer to: here is what I've been working on, and here is where it goes when you follow it.

What to expect

Solo to start.
Conversations when ready.

Early episodes are scripted enough to be precise, spoken enough to stay alive. Solo. Focused. Audio-first.

Over time: researchers, historians, psychologists, writers, technologists, institutional insiders. People worth putting in a room with these ideas.
